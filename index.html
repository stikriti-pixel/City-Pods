How it works

First meeting to move-in

Every project follows the same five steps, so you always know what comes next and what we need from you.

  1. Consultation

    We visit the property, hear your goals and talk through budget range and timing.

  2. Design

    Concept plans, then detailed drawings and selections once the direction is set.

  3. Proposal & permits

    A fixed-price, itemized proposal. We file and track permits and zoning approvals.

  4. Construction

    Your project manager runs the site and sends weekly progress, schedule and budget updates.

  5. Walkthrough

    Final inspections, punch list, closeout documents and your warranty package.

Selections

See the real materials before anything is ordered

During design we walk you through countertop slabs, cabinet door styles and tile, so you choose from the actual materials. Your selections are priced into the proposal before work starts.
